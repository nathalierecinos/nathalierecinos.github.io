Nathalie Recinos

First-Generation College Student | Pre-Business | Finance and Accounting

I am a first-generation college student attending the University of California, Riverside, where I am studying Pre-Business. I graduated from Animo Jackie Robinson Charter High School in 2025. As I continue my college education, I am exploring opportunities in Business Finance and Accounting. I am interested in understanding how businesses manage their finances, make important decisions, and plan for long-term growth. My goal is to gain the knowledge and experience needed to build a successful career in the business field.

My experiences in education, employment, and community service have helped me develop important skills such as communication, teamwork, organization, responsibility, and problem-solving. I enjoy learning new things, helping others, and taking on opportunities that challenge me to improve. I believe that being dependable, willing to learn, and open to new experiences are important qualities in both school and the workplace. I also understand the importance of balancing different responsibilities while staying focused on my goals.

Through my work with Kid City Hope Place, I have had the opportunity to support high school students as they prepare for college. I have helped more than ten students with UC and CSU applications, answered questions about financial aid, and supported college-access activities. I also contributed to curriculum activities for more than thirty high school juniors. This experience helped me understand how important guidance and encouragement can be when students are planning their futures. It strengthened my communication skills and taught me the value of being patient, organized, and supportive when helping others.

I also work at Jetty Coffee Roasters, where I assist customers and contribute to daily café operations. Working in a customer-service environment has helped me practice communication, teamwork, and responsibility. I have learned the importance of being organized, cooperating with coworkers, responding to customer questions, and creating a positive experience for customers. This position has also helped me become more comfortable working in a professional setting.

In addition, I volunteer at St. Francis Center, where I support community activities and help Spanish-speaking families when translation is needed. Being able to communicate in both English and Spanish allows me to help others feel understood and supported. I also volunteer in children's ministry at my church, helping teach and guide children. Working with children and other volunteers has taught me patience, leadership, teamwork, and the importance of creating a welcoming environment.

As a first-generation college student, continuing my education is an important personal goal. College gives me opportunities to explore different career paths, develop new skills, and learn from people with different experiences. I am especially interested in financial management, business operations, accounting, and how organizations make financial decisions. I plan to continue using academic resources, learning from advisors, and gaining practical experience to help me prepare for my future career.

My long-term goal is to build a career in finance or accounting, continue developing professionally, and make a positive contribution wherever I work. I want to become someone who is dependable, knowledgeable, and prepared to take on new responsibilities. I believe that every experience, whether in school, at work, or through volunteering, is an opportunity to learn and grow. I look forward to continuing my education and building a strong foundation for my future.
